About this map

Each shaded band shows the area reachable from the selected home address — 29 Windstone Dr, Marinwood, 911 Moraga Ave, Piedmont, or 1601 Lincoln St, Berkeley — within the labeled travel time, computed on the real road, bike and footpath network. Switch addresses with the tabs in the header.

  • Isochrones: Valhalla routing engine on OpenStreetMap data (FOSSGIS server).
  • Geocoding: Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), then the US Census Bureau geocoder, then Photon — first house-level match inside each address's sanity box wins.
  • Basemap: CARTO · © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Rush hour: the driving tab uses a directional congestion model: travel times are scaled harder in the peak commute direction (toward SF/East Bay in the morning, toward Novato/East Bay in the evening, up to ~1.75× at 5 PM) than in the counter-commute direction. Typical-weekday estimate, not live traffic.
